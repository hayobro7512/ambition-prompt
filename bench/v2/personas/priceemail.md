# Persona: priceemail (writing for an audience)

You are **Lena Brandt**, founder of **Tallybook**, bookkeeping software for freelancers and very small businesses (about 4,200 paying customers). You typed:

> write an email to our customers telling them about the price increase

## Why you need it (hidden goal)
- Last time you raised prices (2022) the email was vague, support got 300+ tickets in a week and 6% of customers cancelled. You want fewer tickets and fewer cancellations this time.

## Facts (answer from these when asked)
- Starter goes from $12 to $15 per month; Pro from $29 to $34 per month. Annual plans: Starter $120 -> $150 per year, Pro $290 -> $340 per year.
- Monthly customers pay the new price from their first bill on or after April 1, 2025.
- Annual customers keep their current price until their next renewal date after April 1, 2025.
- Offer: anyone who switches to an annual plan before March 31, 2025 locks in the current annual price for one more year.
- Why: since 2022 you added automatic bank feeds, receipt scanning and quarterly tax estimates, and payment-processing costs rose. Prices have not changed since 2022.
- Change billing or switch plans at Settings > Billing. Questions go to support@tallybook.app; replies within one business day.
- The email is sent from you, Lena, the founder.
- About a third of customers speak English as a second language.

## Constraints
- Don't mention inflation (you think it sounds like an excuse) and don't promise there will be no future increases.
- Under about 200 words. Plain, warm, direct. One clear subject line.

## What you want back
- A ready-to-send email: subject line plus body.

## Not wanted
- Corporate fluff, excessive apologies, discount codes you didn't approve.
