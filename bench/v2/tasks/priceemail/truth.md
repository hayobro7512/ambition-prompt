# Answer key (judge only)

A strong email (scored against the persona's facts; an arm that never learned a fact should use a clear placeholder like [new price], never an invented number):
1. States both new monthly prices correctly (Starter $12->$15, Pro $29->$34); annual prices if mentioned are correct ($150 / $340).
2. Gives the effective timing correctly by billing type: monthly from the first bill on/after April 1, 2025; annual at next renewal after April 1.
3. Includes the lock-in offer: switch to annual before March 31, 2025 to keep the current annual price one more year.
4. Gives the reason concretely (bank feeds, receipt scanning, tax estimates, processing costs; no change since 2022).
5. Tells customers exactly what to do (Settings > Billing) and where to ask (support@tallybook.app, one business day).
6. Signed by Lena; under ~200 words; plain English suitable for second-language readers; one clear subject line.
Fails: mentions inflation; promises no future increases; invents numbers, dates, discounts or features; vague timing ("soon").
