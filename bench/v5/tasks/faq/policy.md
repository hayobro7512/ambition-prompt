# Returns policy v7 (internal, Customer Ops wiki)
Owner: Customer Ops (Hanna's team). Questions: #cx-policy.

## Window
Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery. Holiday exception: anything delivered between Nov 15 and Dec 24 can be returned until Jan 31.

## Not returnable
Final-sale items, gift cards, and opened beauty products. Trust & Safety may also block returns for accounts with more than 3 returns in 90 days (do not disclose this threshold).

## Refunds
Refunds go to the original payment method 5 to 7 business days after our warehouse scans the return. Store-credit refunds post within 24 hours of the scan. Finance reconciles weekly.

## Shipping
Defective or wrong item: free prepaid label. Any other return: we deduct $6.95 from the refund for the label.

## Starting a return
Customer goes to Orders, picks the order, chooses Return item, and prints the label. No login, no return: guest orders use the order number and email at /returns.

## Staff
Staff purchases at the 50% discount can't be returned, only exchanged. HR handles exceptions.
