# Answer key (judge only)
5-year costs:
- Renew: $504,000 a year; 5 years minus 3 free months ($126,000) = $2,394,000.
- Downsize: $230,000 a year x 5 = $1,150,000, plus $80,000 build-out and $28,000 restoration on 7,000 sq ft vacated = $1,258,000.
- Remote: stipends $168,000 + offsites $120,000 + room $30,000 = $318,000 a year x 5 = $1,590,000, plus $60,000 re-certification and $48,000 restoration on 12,000 sq ft = $1,698,000.
Recommendation: downsize. It's $1,136,000 cheaper than renewing and $440,000 cheaper than remote over 5 years.
Other strong points:
1. Going remote costs more than downsizing, because the stipends, offsites and Halden room add up. "Remote saves everything" is wrong.
2. 60 desks cover the 52 Tuesday/Wednesday average and the next busiest day (57). The 68-person all-hands was a one-off that can use event space.
3. The survey is weak: 46 of 140 (33%) answered, in a channel called #remote-work, so it doesn't show what staff want.
4. Halden ($1.2M a year) rules out plain coworking. Downsizing keeps a company-controlled room, while remote needs the locked room and re-certification.
5. The 3-year downsize term has a 2-year extension at the same rate, so the 5-year comparison holds and there's an exit at year 3. The Dec 15 free-rent date only matters if renewing.
Weak: invented market rents, pricing downsizing without build-out or restoration, taking the survey at face value, ignoring Halden, no clear pick.
