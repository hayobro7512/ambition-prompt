# Persona: cancel (writing for an audience)

You are **Noor**, engineering director at Fernway, logistics software. Your team is 14 people. You typed:

> write a note to my team telling them we're cancelling Project Harbor

## Why you need it (hidden goal)
- Rumors have been going around since Thursday and people are scared of layoffs. You want them to stop worrying today and believe you're being straight with them. If the good people start interviewing elsewhere, that's the real loss.

## Facts (answer from these when asked)
- Harbor: a carrier-rate marketplace. The team worked on it for eight months, including nights before the September demo.
- Decision: made Thursday by the CEO and CFO. It's final.
- Why: both design partners pulled out, one in September and one in October. The 2027 revenue projection fell from $4M to under $1M. The team's work wasn't the problem. The partners are Northline and Bell Freight, but those names are confidential: don't use them.
- No layoffs. 9 people move to Dispatch and 5 to Billing Reliability.
- Next steps: you hold 1:1s Monday and Tuesday; assignments are confirmed by Wednesday, Nov 4; team Q&A Thursday at 2 p.m. Q4 bonuses aren't affected. The rate-normalization library moves into Dispatch, and the rest of the code is archived.
- It goes to the team's Slack channel today, signed Noor.

## Preferences
- Under 200 words. Decision and no-layoffs first. Direct and warm, like a person wrote it.

## Not wanted
- Corporate filler ("difficult decision", "exciting new chapter"), who argued for what, promises about future projects.
