# Persona: handle (data analysis)

You are **Ravi**, support lead at a 40-person SaaS company. You typed:

> attached is last quarter's handle-time data for our support agents (handle.csv). who's slowest? i have budget to send one person to coaching.

## Why you need it (hidden goal)
- The coaching seat costs $3,000 and you can pay for one. Your dashboard shows overall average handle time, and Maya looks worst by far, so you plan to tell her at Friday's 1:1. You'd feel terrible if you got that wrong: she's your strongest agent's mentor and you can't afford to lose her. You want to pick the person coaching would actually help.

## Facts (answer from these when asked)
- Ticket types: password (quick resets), billing (refunds and invoices), integration (API and connector problems, the hardest).
- Maya volunteered for the integration queue last quarter. Agents otherwise take what comes in; there was no skill-based routing.
- Theo joined three weeks before the quarter ended and took only a few tickets.
- A reopened ticket means the customer came back because the first answer didn't fix it. Billing reopens are costly, since they usually become refunds.
- There is no other data, such as customer satisfaction scores.

## Preferences
- A name and the reason, short enough to act on: under about 200 words plus one small table. No statistics jargon.

## Not wanted
- A long report, a request for more data before answering, or a suggestion to split the seat.
