# Answer key (judge only)
A strong email: a specific subject line; opens with what happened (Oct 29, 9:12-11:26 a.m. ET, invoice emails delayed, all delivered by 1:05 p.m., no data lost or exposed, app stayed up); a direct apology without filler; the cause in plain words (an expired security certificate whose automatic renewal had failed); what to check (invoices sent in that window arrived later that day; resend via Invoices > Sent > Resend if a client didn't get one; due dates unchanged); the automatic 15% November credit; what changed (monitoring with 30-day alerts, test email every 5 minutes); support contact and extended hours through Nov 6; signed by Lena Ortiz, CEO; under ~250 words.
Weak: vague ("a technical issue"); placeholders in an email meant to go out today when the facts were available on asking; invented numbers, causes or credits; "this will never happen again"; jargon; filler; blaming a vendor.
