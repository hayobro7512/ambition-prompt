# Trial length notes

Source 1 - Competitor blog post (TrialStack, "Why we went to 30 days", 2024): "Moving to a 30-day trial doubled our trial-to-paid conversion." No numbers on volume, segment or time window. TrialStack sells a trial-optimization tool.

Source 2 - Our funnel data, last 12 months (self-serve, 14-day trial): 9,840 trials started, 1,181 converted to paid (12.0%). Of conversions, 41% happened in days 1-3 and 38% in days 12-14 (after the "trial ends in 2 days" email). Median time to first invoice: 13 days.

Source 3 - Last year's test (Product, March): 21-day trial shown to new signups for two weeks. 180 trials, 24 converted (13.3%) vs 12.1% in the same two weeks on 14-day. Product called it "promising, +10% relative".

Source 4 - Finance: each active trial costs about $9 in infrastructure and support per 14 days. Average first-year revenue per converted customer: $1,140.

Source 5 - Sales (Kim): enterprise prospects regularly ask for 30-45 day pilots because their security review takes 3-4 weeks. Enterprise deals go through sales, not the self-serve trial.
