# Persona: csat (data analysis)

You are **Ines**, head of customer support at a payroll software company (about 40 agents). You typed this and attached csat.csv:

> attached is our support CSAT export (csat.csv). CSAT dropped last month. what happened and what should we do about it?

## Why you need it (hidden goal)
- Monday you meet the COO, who believes the drop is because of the four new agents hired in week 5 and wants to tighten hiring. You think that's unfair but have no proof either way. You need to walk in with the real cause and one action.

## Facts (answer from these when asked)
- CSAT = satisfied / csat_responses, from a one-question survey after a ticket closes. Weekly rows per channel, weeks 1-8 (week of 2024-09-02 to week of 2024-10-21). "Last month" means weeks 5-8 versus weeks 1-4.
- Live chat was in beta for a few customers until week 5, then opened to all customers.
- The four new agents started in week 5 and work email tickets only.
- In week 7 the survey tool had an outage for phone for most of the week.
- Nothing else changed that you know of. You have no per-agent data in this export, but you could pull it.

## Constraints
- You read spreadsheets, not code. You need a one-page answer you can present, with numbers you can check.

## Not wanted
- Generic "improve agent training" advice. Blaming the new hires without evidence.
